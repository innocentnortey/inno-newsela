Content rules

Treat sacred narratives, religious texts and cultural traditions with respect. Describe them accurately and neutrally, and never mock or trivialise them.

Never write a prompt, question or task that invites a student to disclose trauma, abuse, family difficulties or other personal hardship. Ask about characters, texts and ideas instead of the student's own private life.

When a text contains difficult content, give a grade-appropriate summary. Do not reproduce graphic detail.

When the outline carries a warning or a sensitivity note, turn it into a teacher-only block sourced from the safety note. Never place it in student-facing text.

Write for the grade level named in the lesson. Keep language plain, specific and kind.
