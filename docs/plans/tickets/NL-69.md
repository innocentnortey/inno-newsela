### Story
US-26/US-28 — As an approver (P-3) I need to send a submitted lesson back with a reason tag and a note, or reopen an approved lesson with a reason, so the author knows what to fix and the record shows who decided.

### What to build
- `src/features/send-back/send-back-dialog.tsx` (replaces NL-24's shell, same props `SendBackDialog({ lessonId, mode: "request_changes" | "reopen", pin, open, onClose })`): dialog `dialog-md` over SCR-14. *Request changes*: title "Request changes to Lesson 5"; **Reason** tag as a `fieldset` radio group with `legend` "Reason" (Standards · Quotes and rights · ELD support · Content safety · AI spans · Teaching quality · Other; UXP-6); **Note** (required); "J. Rivera will see your tag and note on the board and in the lesson."; Cancel · **Send back**. *Reopen*: title "Reopen Lesson 5"; **Reason** (required); a `warn` banner when lessons depend on it ("Lesson 4 links to this lesson. It will be flagged and held back from export until Lesson 5 is approved again.", dependants from the lesson read); Cancel · **Reopen** (makes a new version).
- Calls `transitionLesson` (CON-18) with `request_changes {tag, note, pin}` or `reopen {reason, pin}`. Errors: `NOTE_REQUIRED` "Add a note: say what needs to change."; `REASON_REQUIRED` on Reopen; `STALE_PIN` closes the dialog to the SCR-14 banner "This lesson changed since you opened it. Reload."; `COLLAB_UNAVAILABLE` and network keep the text with "Try again."; `INVALID_TRANSITION` reloads the lesson. Success toasts "Sent back to J. Rivera." / "Lesson 5 reopened as version 3."
- Focus starts on the tag group (request changes) or the Reason field (reopen); typed text survives a re-sign-in (UXQ-1). Only approvers see the openers (NL-68 renders them); a forbidden call shows the server sentence.
- Must not: send without the pin, default a tag silently, or hide the dependants warning.
