### Story
US-13 / US-14 / US-29 — As a reviewer, author or approver I open a lesson and see everything the workspace and the approval review need in one answer (content, standards with who asserted them, texts, outline row, checklist, dependencies), and I can read its full history: every version, its provenance and every audited action.

### What to build
Implement `LessonReadService` in `apps/web/src/server/services/lesson-read-service.ts` (DO-4 §5) and the two routes. Read-only: it opens no transaction and writes nothing, not even an audit row (INTERFACE shared guarantee 5).
**`get_lesson(user, lesson_id)`** (`getLesson`, `apps/web/app/api/lessons/[lessonId]/route.ts`): accepts `G6.U4.L4` or `g6-u4:L4` (UXQ-2, normalised by the id parser in `packages/schema`); `states.get` (`NOT_FOUND`); reads the **derived row** (`LessonDocReader.latest_derived`) for editable states and the **latest snapshot** (`LessonVersionRepo.latest_snapshot`) for `submitted`, `approved`, `exported`; `assert_known_nodes` over the content → `SchemaUnknownError` → `SCHEMA_UNKNOWN` (500, `details` names the lesson and schema version; nothing dropped). Builds the CON-11 response: ids/keys, `state`, `version_number`, `content_hash`, `state_vector`, `schema_version`, `last_editor`; `snapshot{version_id, content_hash, state_vector}` for submitted and later; `front_matter.standards[]` with `origin`, `official_text`, `asserted_by` and `confirmation` (from `AlignmentRepo`, official CDE text only); `sections` with addresses; `text_cards`; `outline_row` with segments (from `OutlineRepo`); `alignment_map`; `reviewer`; `send_back`; `dependencies`; `checklist` for submitted and later (keys `standards`, `quotes_and_rights`, `eld_support`, `content_safety`, `ai_spans`, `dependencies`, the ones that apply, status `ok|open`, `evidence_refs`); and `allowed_actions` as a hint from `transition_roles`/`decide_transition` (NL-21) — `start_review` only for a reviewer on a `draft` (UXQ-11). The constructor includes `issues: IssueRepo`, `flags: SafetyFlagRepo`, `graph: LessonGraphRepo` for the checklist and dependency lines (DO-4 §5 `LessonReadService`, §13 R-6). All text is returned as text, never HTML (R-58); no minutes or person-level metric appears (GR-15).
**`history(user, lesson_id)`** (`getLessonHistory`, `…/history/route.ts`): `versions` (id, number, kind with `approve` shown as `approval`, hash, created_at/by, `adopted`, provenance — every version non-null; human-made versions inherit and show empty model fields, cost 0), `audit` from `AuditLog.history` (view `audit_log_v`), `ai_spans` (accepted by, at).
Must not: write anything, read the live document, edit `app/api/lessons/[lessonId]/` folders other than `route.ts` and `history/**`, or fill `wiring/review.ts` (NL-53).
