### Story
US-17/US-18 — As a reviewer (P-1) I need to fill a quote placeholder or an excerpt slot with real text, its source and its rights status, so every quote in an approved lesson is a person's sourced choice.

### What to build
- `src/features/insert-quote/insert-quote-dialog.tsx` (replaces NL-24's shell, same props `InsertQuoteDialog({ lessonId, slotId, open, onClose })`) and its form parts: dialog `dialog-lg` over SCR-9; eyebrow with the address (`G6.U4.L4.LearningInAction.PartA`); title "Insert quote" or "Fill excerpt slot" by slot kind; a `well` quoting what the slot asks for; **Text** textarea with the counter "9 of 12 words" while rights are `short-quote-commentary`; **Source** (book, chapter, page; or URL for an excerpt); **Rights status** select (`short-quote-commentary`, `public-domain`, `licensed`, `to-clear`) with one-line help per option; Cancel · **Insert**.
- Submits `fillSlot` (CON-15). Errors shown on the field: `SOURCE_REQUIRED` "A quote needs a source: add the book and page, or the URL."; `QUOTE_TOO_LONG` "13 of 12 words: a short-quote-commentary quote is 12 words or fewer. Shorten it or choose another rights status."; `LESSON_LOCKED`, `COLLAB_UNAVAILABLE` ("Couldn't insert. Your text is kept. Try again."), `ROLE_FORBIDDEN` "Only reviewers insert quotes." The counter is a client hint only; the server's word rule decides. Success closes the dialog, toast "Quote inserted. Issue 1 resolved."; rights `to-clear`: "Inserted. Still blocking: rights to clear."
- Typed text kept through a re-sign-in on the same page via NL-24's `draft-memory.ts` (UXQ-1) and never written elsewhere before **Insert**. Insert is not offered on frozen lessons (the slot shows no button; NL-62 owns that rendering). Focus starts in Text; the counter announces only on crossing the limit.
- Must not: count words as the authority, trim or alter the quote, write the document (the server applies it through the collab server), or render the quote as HTML.
