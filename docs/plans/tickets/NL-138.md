### What to build
**What Innocent does (`gate::human`).** About 60–90 minutes across two or three rounds.
1. **Read the script** (`docs/delivery/*-demo-script.md`, NL-136) and say "script OK" or list changes. Check that every line is something he would say and defend.
2. **Confirm the voice key** is in `.demo/.env` (NL-03). Claude never prints it.
3. **Claude produces the cut** with the `demo-video` skill against the release-candidate stack at the NL-137 SHA: it drives the browser with a visible cursor, voices the narration with ElevenLabs, and assembles the MP4 with ffmpeg, captions and title cards. The lesson shown is picked at random with the method named in the script (GR-14); one caught error is shown; every number on screen shows its *n*.
4. **Innocent watches the whole video once** and answers "approved" or lists changes (at most two further rounds; each is a re-cut of the affected shots).
5. **Where it lives.** He decides where reviewers watch it (an unlisted link he controls) and gives Claude the URL for `SUBMISSION.md`; the file is also kept at `docs/artifacts/<YYYY-MM-DD>-demo-video.mp4` (not committed if over the repository's size policy; then the path is recorded and the file stays on the VPS).

**What Claude records afterwards.** The SHA the video shows, the duration of the product segment and the total, the file path and checksum, the URL he gave, his words ("script OK", "approved"), the dev-log entry and `.claude/state/resume.md` next action (NL-141). Claude does not publish the video anywhere and does not alter a narrated number.
