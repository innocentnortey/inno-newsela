### Story
US-34 — As a content lead (P-3), I need … the seeded-error catch rate, each with its *n*, so that I can judge the drafts and the review, not just the speed. M-4 (G-3) is the guard on the north star: reviewer minutes only mean something if review still catches planted errors. The test is only fair if the reviewer does not know where the errors are (R-52).

### What to build
**What Claude does first (the planter; `npm run eval:seed`, NL-92).** Plan about 5 errors on copies of lessons from the NL-112 unit, of different kinds, including the **"wrong code" plant** (a focus-standard code outside the row's lists, which must be caught: the S10 floor) and, for example, an invented quote, a page number out of range, a missing teaching link, minutes outside 50 ± 5. No model call is needed: `npm run eval:seed -- --plan <hidden key>`. The key (which lesson, which error) is written to the git-ignored `evals/local/seed-key.json` and **is never shown to Innocent or placed in the issue** until the run ends.

**What Innocent does (blind review; `gate::human`).** About 45–60 minutes. Claude gives him one sentence: "Review these lessons as you normally would and approve what you would approve; do not look for anything in particular." Then:
1. Sign in as the **reviewer** account; open each lesson in the seeded set from the review queue (SCR-9), read, fix what you would fix, resolve what the checks raise, and **Submit** or **Request changes** exactly as you would for real.
2. Sign in as the **approver** account (not the last editor): open each submitted lesson (SCR-14) and **Approve** or **Send back**. Do not skip a lesson because it "looks planted"; the number only means something if you treat each as real.
3. Say "seeded review done" on the issue. Do not open `evals/local/` and do not ask how many there were.

**What Claude records afterwards.** Only after "done": unseal the key, match each planted error to what happened (caught by a check, caught by the reviewer, caught at approval, or **missed and approved**), and hand the scoring to NL-135. Claude plants the errors and never reviews or approves them (the planter is not the reviewer); the synthetic accounts are not used for the review.
