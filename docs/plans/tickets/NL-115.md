### Story
US-24…US-28 and US-31 — As a reviewer I submit a lesson when nothing blocks it; as an approver, a second person, I approve one pinned version on its evidence and rate how usable the draft was; I can send a lesson back with a reason, approve linked lessons in order, and reopen an approved lesson without breaking its dependants. This is the product's central promise (R-33, HP-5), proved on the real stack.

### What to build
Drive WF-15 end to end with Playwright and API tests on the Compose stack (stub provider, two browser contexts for two accounts) and fix the seams on SCR-14, SCR-15 and SCR-9's submit action.

- Owns: `apps/web/e2e/integration/con-18/**`, `apps/web/src/features/workspace/actions/submit/**`, SCR-14 (Approval review) and SCR-15 (Send back or reopen) and their TRACEABILITY rows (SCR-9 row: append only).
- Flows: `start_review` and `submit` (web checks role and state → collaboration internal freeze: lock, store at once, state-vector cover check, blocking checks on the stored row, snapshot + state + transition in one transaction, `read_only` mode); `withdraw`; `approve` by a different account pinned to the snapshot hash and state vector with a rating (re-checks the snapshot, writes approval snapshot, audit row, and at the first approval the frozen metrics row once); `request_changes`; `reopen` (new version from the approved snapshot).
- Error precedence per CON-18: `ROLE_FORBIDDEN` → `NOT_FOUND` → `INVALID_TRANSITION` → `NOTE_REQUIRED` / `REASON_REQUIRED` / `RATING_REQUIRED` → `NOT_STORED` → `STALE_PIN` → `FOUR_EYES` → `SUGGESTION_PENDING` → `BLOCKED` → `CONFIRMATION_OPEN` → `DEPENDENCY_UNAPPROVED`.
- **risk::security:** a bug here approves content nobody reviewed or lets one person approve their own work. `test-writer-agent` freezes AC-3 to AC-8 and AC-11 as failing tests before the implementer starts (frozen-test manifest and tamper check apply); `security-privacy-agent` reads this diff at the wave merge.
- Must not: add any route taking more than one lesson, edit `decide_transition` (NL-21), the freeze command (NL-106) or the metrics writer (NL-48) — defects found there go back to their tickets.
