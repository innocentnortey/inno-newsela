### What to build
Fill the bodies of the grounding rule files NL-20 stubbed. Pure functions; port the scan logic from the SP-3 harness (`spike/SP-3`, `spikes/sp3/harness`; do not import it) with the values SP-3 settled: **quote span ≥ 3 words**, quote and page scans **blocking in every block role**, name scan from the **capitalised-token heuristic plus `compromise`**, blocking **only inside `cfu_item` options and rationales**, advisory elsewhere (`CheckPolicy.name_scan_scope = "blocking_in_cfu_items"`).
**`packages/core/src/checks/rules/grounding/`** — `quote_unmatched` (blocking: any quotation-mark span of ≥ `policy.quote_span_min_words` words, a word being a token with a letter or digit, that is not a substring of the lesson's own row cells, `ctx.arc_header`, a `ctx.text_cards` title or the official `ctx.standards` text; titles in quotes and the row's own quoted words pass); `page_out_of_range` (blocking: a page **or chapter** reference outside the row's range, read from the text card/segment); `proper_noun_unsourced` (a proper noun not in the row, arc header, text cards or standards text, minus a routine/section-name allow-list kept in `grounding/allow-list.ts`; per-finding `level` = `blocking` inside a `cfu_item` option or rationale, `advisory` elsewhere); `quote_without_source` (blocking: a `quote` node with no `source_ref`); `quote_too_long` (blocking: a reviewer-inserted `quote` with rights `short-quote-commentary` over `policy.reviewer_quote_max_words` = 12 words); `rights_to_clear` (blocking: a `quote`/excerpt with rights `to-clear`, GR-3); `quote_placeholder_open` and `excerpt_slot_open` (blocking: a `text_evidence_needed` block or an excerpt slot still unfilled; `address` of the block). A sentence split across ProseMirror text nodes is joined before the span scan.
Must not: invent a threshold (read `policy`), reach the network or a model, treat the name scan as blocking outside `cfu_item`s, edit NL-20's files or another group's folder, or copy any text from `docs/source/restricted/` into fixtures (seeded copies are built from SP-1's drafts and SYN data only, GR-9).
