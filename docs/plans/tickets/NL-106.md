### Story
US-24 — As a reviewer I submit a lesson when nothing is blocking, to freeze it, so that the approver reads exactly what I sent. The promise (HP-5): after the lock, zero edits are persisted and every keystroke is either in the snapshot or reported as refused to its typist.

### What to build
Fill `apps/collab/src/commands/lock-and-snapshot.ts` (NL-07 created the shell; NL-105, which this ticket now depends on, builds `CollabCommandHandler` in `routes.ts` and adds the typed `lock_and_snapshot` export that refuses with `COLLAB_UNAVAILABLE`; replace that body): `CollabCommandHandler.lock_and_snapshot(SubmitCommand) -> SnapshotResult`. **SP-2 option A**, in this order and no other:

1. `FrozenDocs.freeze(lesson_id)` **first** — from here `WriteGuard.on_update` refuses every update with `refused` / `refused_from`.
2. Store at once (`LessonPersistence.store`, no debounce): the live Y.Doc → one `lesson_doc` row.
3. Cover check: the stored `state_vector` must cover `client_state_vector` (`covers`), else `FrozenDocs.unfreeze` and `NOT_STORED`.
4. `CheckContextLoader.load` + `run_checks(CHECK_RULES, CHECK_POLICY)` **on the stored row**, then `merge_resolutions(findings, IssueRepo.resolutions_for(lesson_id))`, then `submit_gate` in CON-18's order: `SUGGESTION_PENDING`, `BLOCKED` (`details.issues` lists every open blocking **and** confirmation issue), `CONFIRMATION_OPEN`; on any refusal `unfreeze` and refuse.
5. One transaction: lock the lesson row, re-check `decide_transition`, write `lesson_version` (kind `submit`, with the stored hash and state vector), `lesson.state = submitted`, `review_event`. Commit, then `AckBroadcaster.mode(read_only, submitted)`.
6. On a refusal at any step the lesson is unfrozen again and nothing is persisted from steps 4–5.

There is **no close-every-connection path** (SP-2 rejected option B: refused keystrokes came back on reconnect, 120 of them in 20 trials). The web side (`TransitionService.submit`, NL-53) checks role and state and passes the refusals through unchanged. A process crash between 1 and 5 loses only the in-memory freeze; a restart re-seeds `FrozenDocs` from `lesson.state`, which is still editable.

**Must not:** persist any update received after step 1; write `submitted` without the snapshot in the same transaction; broadcast `read_only` before commit; edit `routes.ts`, NL-80 or NL-105 files other than the refusing `lock_and_snapshot` body in `lock-and-snapshot.ts`.
