### Story
US-22 — As a reviewer (P-1) I need a regenerated candidate shown beside the current lesson, section by section, so I can adopt the whole candidate, one section, or leave it, without losing the current lesson.

### What to build
- `app/(app)/units/[unitKey]/lessons/[lessonKey]/candidates/[candidate]/page.tsx` + `src/features/candidate/**`: breadcrumb "… › Lesson 4 › Candidate v4"; `h1` "Candidate v4 · not adopted" with meta (from the run's stored plan and pinned prompt, the note); two page sheets (current | candidate) aligned by section, each section row with **Adopt this section**; header **Adopt whole candidate** (primary) and **Leave unadopted** (ghost, back to SCR-9); under `lg` the sheets become tabs *Current · Candidate*; the note "Copying text by hand from the candidate is not tracked as an adoption." (HP-5 limit). `?section=` scrolls to a section.
- Data: `getCandidate` (CON-16), polled through `use-poll.ts` while drafting ("Drafting a candidate… The current lesson is unchanged." with the tile pill), `adoptCandidate` for whole or one section. Sections render through NL-62's read-view components (import, do not copy); all text as text (R-58).
- States: failed ("Candidate failed: Look Back is 3 minutes (needs 5). The current lesson is unchanged."); `DAY_CAP_REACHED` "Daily spend cap reached. No candidate was made."; `LESSON_LOCKED` "Lesson 4 is submitted. Withdraw it first to regenerate."; `INVALID_TRANSITION` reloads and shows the real state; `COLLAB_UNAVAILABLE` "Nothing changed. Try again."; partial (a section shows "Adopted by J. Rivera"); success toast "Look Back adopted from candidate v4. Logged as your edit." and return to SCR-9 with checks re-running; non-reviewers read only (no adopt controls, with "Only reviewers adopt candidates").
- Must not: merge text on the client, adopt a candidate that is not `ready`, or offer adoption on a frozen lesson. The client never edits the lesson; adoption is the server's single write (collab-applied).
