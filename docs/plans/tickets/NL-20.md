### What to build
`packages/core/src/checks/` (A6a; pure; `compromise` is allow-listed for NL-40 only).
**`types.ts`.** `CheckFinding`, `CheckResult`, `CheckRule`, `CheckContext`, `GateState`, `GateIssue`, `CheckRuleList` (DO-4 §8). Heavy members of `CheckContext` (`standards`, `linked_lessons`, `pending_suggestions`, `safety_flags`, `confirmations`, `links`) and the recorded-resolution input of `merge_resolutions` are typed with narrow **structural** shapes that the richer DO-4 records satisfy (`StandardRow`, `DerivedRow`, `SuggestionRow`, `SafetyFlagRow`, `AlignmentLinkRow`, `IssueRow`, `LessonLinkRecord` arrive in NL-12/NL-38 a wave later); `lesson: StoredLesson`, `row: NormalizedRow` come from `@ls/schema`.
**`policy.ts`.** `CheckPolicy`, frozen `CHECK_POLICY` with the SP-3 values: `quote_span_min_words 3`, `reviewer_quote_max_words 12`, `quote_and_page_scans "blocking_everywhere"`, `name_scan_scope "blocking_in_cfu_items"`, `name_scan_engine "heuristic_plus_compromise"`, `minutes_target 50`, `minutes_tolerance 5`, `vocabulary_cap 6`, `safety_run_min_words 6`.
**`registry.ts`.** `CHECK_RULES`: exactly one rule per `CheckKind` (28), in `CheckKind` order (DO-4 §2). Rule files live at `rules/<group>/<kind>.ts`; each group's `index.ts` exports its `RULES`. **This ticket writes all 28 files**: the bodies of the four structural kinds, and stubs (`run` returns `[]`, `level` set) for the rest, so no later ticket edits `registry.ts`. Group map (decided here): `structural` — `schema_invalid`, `section_missing`, `minutes_out_of_range`, `vocabulary_word_missing` (blocking, PRD R-19 as amended, U2; DO-4 §13 R-8); `alignment` (NL-39) — `standard_not_taught`, `standard_not_checked`, `code_not_allowed`, `eld_label_mismatch`, `standard_confirmation`, `standard_add_suggestion`, `judge_no`; `safety` (NL-39) — `safety_flag_open`, `safety_note_without_tip`, `safety_text_in_student_block`, `disclosure_prompt`; `links` (NL-39) — `cross_lesson_link`, `linked_text_changed`, `suggestion_pending`; `grounding` (NL-40) — `quote_placeholder_open`, `excerpt_slot_open`, `quote_unmatched`, `page_out_of_range`, `proper_noun_unsourced`, `quote_without_source`, `quote_too_long`, `rights_to_clear`; `arc` (NL-41) — `arc_repeat`, `vocabulary_over_cap`. `rules/alignment/derive.ts` is a stub `derive_alignment(ctx): AlignmentLinkRow[]` returning `[]` (NL-39 fills it). `run_checks(ctx, rules, policy): CheckResult` runs every rule, orders findings by `CheckKind` order then `address`, counts `blocking` / `confirmation` / `advisory` by level, sets `first_failing_kind` to the first kind (in `CheckKind` order) with a blocking finding, and never swallows a rule's exception. Levels for the filled rules follow CON-13 and PRD R-19 as amended (§5.4): `standard_confirmation` is `confirmation`; `eld_label_mismatch`, `arc_repeat`, `vocabulary_over_cap`, `judge_no` are `advisory`; the rest (including `vocabulary_word_missing`) `blocking`; stubs take the level their owning ticket will confirm.
**`gates.ts`.** `merge_resolutions(findings, recorded): GateIssue[]` (resolved when a recorded resolution has the same `(kind, address, code)` and the kind is resolvable; resolutions never carry to a finding of another key; non-resolvable kinds: `suggestion_pending`, `standard_confirmation`, `safety_flag_open`, `safety_note_without_tip`, `safety_text_in_student_block`, `rights_to_clear`, `cross_lesson_link`: CON-13). `submit_gate(state)` → `GateDecision` with `refusals` in order `SUGGESTION_PENDING`, `BLOCKED` (unresolved blocking issues; `details.issues` lists blocking **and** confirmation), `CONFIRMATION_OPEN`; `approve_gate(state)` → `FOUR_EYES` (when enabled and `last_editor_id == approver_id`), `SUGGESTION_PENDING`, `BLOCKED`, `CONFIRMATION_OPEN`, `DEPENDENCY_UNAPPROVED` (`details` names the source lesson). `allowed` is true only with no refusal.
**Structural rules (bodies).** `schema_invalid`: heading levels never skip (an h2 followed by h4), every table has a header row, every CFU item has exactly one option marked Correct and a rationale for each option (R-58, R-15). `section_missing`: a template section absent, or a section lacking a `required_role` (including the three Integrated ELD callouts in Literacy Lab and Learning in Action, R-14). `minutes_out_of_range`: a section's `minutes` differs from the template's by more than `minutes_tolerance`, or the lesson total is outside 50 ± 5. `vocabulary_word_missing`: a word of `ctx.row.vocabulary` found in neither of the lesson's two vocabulary lists (U2: "Addressed in Lesson" = `front_matter.vocabulary`, and the "for Additional Practice" list in the `personal_dictionary` section), compared case-insensitively after trimming; one finding per missing word at the front-matter address. Addresses on findings are section addresses or `<section>.Part<X>`.
Must not: fill a non-structural rule body (NL-39/40/41), edit another ticket's rule file after this merge, read a clock or the network, or import a driver.
