### Story
US-17 / US-18 — As a reviewer I replace a "Reviewer to insert" placeholder or an excerpt slot with a real quote from a source I verified, with its source and rights status, so that the lesson's evidence is real and V-3 (100% of quotes sourced) holds.

### What to build
Implement `SlotService` in `apps/web/src/server/services/slot-service.ts` (DO-4 §5) and the route `apps/web/app/api/lessons/[lessonId]/slots/[slotId]/fill/route.ts` (`fillSlot`, role `reviewer`, 200).
`fill(user, lesson_id, slot_id, req)` in CON-15's precedence: role → lesson `NOT_FOUND` → `LESSON_LOCKED` (state not editable per `is_editable`, nothing sent) → missing-input checks **before any edit**: `SOURCE_REQUIRED` (422: `source_ref` has none of `book`/`title`, `chapter`, `pages`, `url`) and `QUOTE_TOO_LONG` (422: rights `short-quote-commentary` with more than `CheckPolicy.reviewer_quote_max_words` = 12 words; a word is a token with a letter or digit, punctuation-only tokens do not count) → `CollabCommands.apply_edit(EditCommand{edit: SlotFillEdit{slot_id, text, source_ref, rights_status}, tag "slot-fill", actor: the reviewer})`. The collaboration server inserts the `quote` node as plain text in one Yjs transaction and stores at once; this service returns after that store: `slot_id`, `inserted_by`, `inserted_at`, `content_hash` (the stored hash from `StoreAck`), `still_blocking` (true when rights are `to-clear`, with `blocking_reason "rights to clear"`), and `linked_lessons` (lessons whose link this slot feeds with their new status, e.g. waiting on Lesson 5's approval, UXP-2). A collab refusal passes through with its own code (`LESSON_LOCKED`, `NOT_FOUND` for a slot a co-editor deleted); transport failure → `COLLAB_UNAVAILABLE`, nothing inserted. Re-filling a filled slot replaces the quote (how a `to-clear` is later recorded as cleared). Additive constructor change for `linked_lessons`, to record in the dev log: `graph: LessonGraphRepo`.
Must not: validate by writing, touch lesson content from the web process (CON-G3), accept text over 10,000 characters (OpenAPI `maxLength`, `INVALID_REQUEST`), render text as HTML, or edit another `lessons/[lessonId]/` folder.
