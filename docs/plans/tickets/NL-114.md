### Story
US-22 — As a reviewer I need to ask for a fresh draft without losing my edits: the new draft arrives beside the current lesson as a candidate, and I adopt the whole or one section as a logged edit. This ticket proves it on the real routes, worker and collaboration server.

### What to build
Drive WF-13 end to end (Playwright and API tests on the Compose stack, stub provider) and fix the seams on SCR-12 (Candidate compare) and SCR-9's regenerate entry.

- Owns: `apps/web/e2e/integration/con-16/**`, `apps/web/src/features/workspace/actions/regenerate/**`, SCR-12 and its TRACEABILITY row (shared SCR-9 row: append only).
- Flow: `regenerateLesson` (optional note) → one-lesson job on the run's stored plan and pinned prompt → candidate `lesson_version` marked "not adopted" → `getCandidate` polled every 2 s → `adoptCandidate` whole or section → collaboration server applies a `candidate-adopt` edit by the named person; the adopter becomes last editor.
- Spend: the candidate has its own per-lesson cap, counts against the requester's day cap and the project ceiling, never the original run's cap.
- Must not: overwrite an edited, in-review or approved lesson; edit the job runner (A9), collaboration commands (NL-105) or checks.
