### Story
US-23 — As a reviewer I can ask the AI to rewrite a passage and decide myself: the suggestion waits outside the document, and only my accept, applied by the collaboration server, changes the lesson, tagged `ai-assist` with my name. Cuttable (#8): if cut, close unbuilt and the docs say no AI assist shipped.

### What to build
Fill the web assist wiring and prove WF-14 end to end on the real stack with the stub model.

- Owns: `apps/web/src/server/wiring/assist.ts` (builds `SuggestionService` with the real `PaidCaller`, `PromptRenderer.render_assist`, `SuggestionRepo`, `CollabCommands`), `apps/web/e2e/integration/con-17/**`, SCR-13 (AI assist panel) and its TRACEABILITY row.
- Flow: `requestSuggestion` (anchor, ≤ 2,000-char `selected_text`, note) → synchronous stub call (20 s timeout) → `pending` suggestion held outside the document; `listSuggestions` after reload; `decideSuggestion` accept → collaboration server resolves the anchor against the live document, compares its text with the stored `selected_text`, replaces it in one transaction tagged `ai-assist` attributed to the acceptor, stores; reject → discarded and logged; a pending suggestion closes itself after 30 min.
- **risk::security:** untrusted model text enters a document. `test-writer-agent` freezes AC-3, AC-4, AC-6, AC-7 and AC-8 first; `security-privacy-agent` reads the diff at the wave merge.
- Must not: apply an accept from the client, trust `anchored_text_now`, offer assist on an approved lesson, or send anything but the selected text, note and the lesson's own context to the model.
