### Story
US-25/US-27/US-31 — As an approver (P-3) I need to read one submitted lesson exactly as frozen, check each checklist item against its evidence, rate the draft, and approve that pinned version, one lesson at a time.

### What to build
- `app/(app)/units/[unitKey]/lessons/[lessonKey]/approve/page.tsx` + `src/features/approval/**`: breadcrumb *Unit 4 › Approvals › Lesson 5*; stepper on step 3; eyebrow "Approval review"; meta with the pin icon and the snapshot hash `9f3c…a21e`; dependency banner ("Lesson 4 is waiting on this lesson's excerpt."); the **Checklist** (4–6 items, each with a check/blocking glyph, bold title, evidence line and a link that jumps to its span in the frozen text) from `getLesson` (CON-11 checklist and snapshot) and `getLessonIssues` (CON-13 re-checked); **Lesson text (frozen at `9f3c…a21e`)** through NL-62's read-view components. Checklist items load before the frozen text (skeleton for the text).
- Decision rail: four-eyes banner (`success` "Four eyes · You did not edit this lesson, so you can approve it." / `warn` "Someone else must approve: you were the last editor of this lesson (including AI accepts and adopted candidates)."), the `fieldset` with `legend` "Draft quality (required)" and radio cards (Usable as drafted · Usable with edits · Rewritten), **Approve this version** (primary `lg`, `aria-disabled` with its reason), **Request changes** (secondary `lg`, opens SCR-15 / NL-69), and the pin small print. Approve sends `transitionLesson` with the pinned hash and state vector and the rating.
- Errors as spec: `STALE_PIN` 409 → `role="alert"` banner "This lesson changed since you opened it. Reload." with **Reload**, focus on it, the decision card off until reload; `RATING_REQUIRED` → "Choose a rating to approve." and focus on the first radio; `FOUR_EYES`; `DEPENDENCY_UNAPPROVED` ("Homework text — Lesson 5 must be approved first" with a link; Approve off with that reason); `BLOCKED` ("Approve refused: `RL.6.2` has no checking item.", the item flips to blocking); network ("Couldn't reach the server. Your rating is kept. Try again."). Not submitted: "Lesson 4 isn't submitted yet. It's in review with M. Okafor." with a read-only link and no decision card.
- Success: `success` banner "Approved by D. Park · pinned `9f3c…a21e` · Usable with edits", stepper step 4, toast "Lesson 5 approved." and, if a lesson waits on it, "Lesson 4 can now be approved."; `approved`/`exported` replace the card with "Approved by D. Park on 2026-10-25" and **Reopen…** (SCR-15). Reviewer/author: everything readable, the card says "Only approvers can approve". Heartbeats (CON-20) use NL-64's hook; no bulk control exists.
- Must not: approve without the pin, hide the rating requirement, compute eligibility client-side beyond presenting the server's reasons, or offer any multi-lesson action (GR-6).
