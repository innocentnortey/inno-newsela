### Story
US-23 — As a reviewer (P-1) I need to rewrite a selected passage from my note as a suggestion I accept or reject, so AI help is visible, optional and never silent (cut #8: the whole panel can be dropped).

### What to build
- `src/features/assist/assist-panel.tsx` (replaces NL-24's shell, same props `AssistPanel({ lessonId, selection, onClose })`): a panel that replaces the rail's content while open — title "Rewrite selection", a back arrow to the rail, the selected text quoted, **My note**, a `warn` line "Don't paste student work.", **Ask for a suggestion** (secondary, never a sparkle), and the suggestion card (look-sheet anatomy `d1-05`): the rewrite, **Accept** (primary) and **Reject**. `requestSuggestion` then `decideSuggestion` (CON-17); `listSuggestions` restores a pending suggestion after a reload.
- States: while pending, the panel tells NL-64's bar via the shared `clientState` channel so Submit and Approve show "Accept or reject the AI suggestion first"; `ANCHOR_CHANGED` "Text changed, ask again."; `MODEL_UNAVAILABLE` "Couldn't get a suggestion. Nothing changed."; `LESSON_LOCKED`/frozen: not offered; `INVALID_TRANSITION` re-reads; `COLLAB_UNAVAILABLE` leaves the suggestion pending with a retry; accepted: toast "Applied as your edit (AI-assisted)."; rejected: "Discarded."
- Cut #8: the panel is reachable only through the More-menu entry NL-64 shows when NL-24's nav/cut flag stands; with the flag off nothing here is reachable and the build still passes.
- Must not: apply text itself (the server applies an accepted suggestion through the collab server), auto-accept, offer assist on frozen lessons, or render the rewrite as HTML.
