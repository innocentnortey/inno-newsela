### Story
Platform ticket (cut #2): the judge may only be reported once it agrees with Innocent's own labels (agreement ≥ 0.8 on at least 20 labels, confirmed on 10 held back). This script measures that; the labels come from NL-122 and the written record from NL-132.

### What to build
Write `npm run eval:judge-calibrate` and fill the S12 suite shell.

- Input: `evals/judge/labels.csv` (NL-122, Innocent's labels): `pair_id, lesson_id, content_hash, address, code, question, label (yes|no), labelled_by, labelled_at, split (dev|holdout)`. A missing file, fewer than 20 dev labels, or a holdout of fewer than 10 → exit non-zero with a message; no judge call is made.
- For each pair the script loads the activity text from the stored lesson (`LessonDocReader` at `lesson_id` and `content_hash`) and the standard text (`StandardRepo`), builds a `JudgeRequest` and calls the **same** `JudgeClient` the product uses (stub by default; `OpenRouterJudgeAdapter` only with `--real --cap-usd <n>` at most `BUILD_SPEND_CEILING_USD`), recording every call as a `model_call` (`purpose "judge"`) under a `SpendLedger` reservation.
- Output: one `eval_case_result` per pair (`provenance issued` label, source `labels.csv:<pair_id>`, expected = label, actual = verdict) and one `eval_run`; `evals/reports/judge-calibration.md` with agreement on the dev labels and *n*, the agreement on the 10 holdout labels, the judge model and prompt/rubric version, and a line `judge verdicts may be reported: yes|no` that is **yes only when dev agreement ≥ 0.8 on ≥ 20 labels and the holdout confirms** (also ≥ 0.8). Any change to the judge prompt or model re-runs it.

**Must not:** make the judge a gate for Approve; tune the rubric against the holdout split; read the holdout labels before the dev agreement is computed and written; spend without a cap; put label data in the repo beyond `labels.csv` (no student data exists in it).
