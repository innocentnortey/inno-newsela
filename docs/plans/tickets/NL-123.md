### Story
US-30 — As a content lead (P-3), I need the board's north-star tile to show the median active minutes per approved lesson with its *n* …, so that the number can't be read as a saving it isn't. US-25 and US-31 add the approve-on-the-pinned-version step and the one-click rating. Only Innocent's own reviews are human sessions (PD-25, amended); every Claude-driven session is `synthetic` and excluded. These sessions give the north-star tile its first honest numbers and S8's stopwatch check its reference.

### What to build
**What Innocent does (only he can review as a human; `gate::human`).** About 60–75 minutes in all, in the real app on the release-candidate stack.
1. **Setup (Claude, before).** Stack up from the committed Compose config with the NL-112 unit loaded; two demo accounts exist, **reviewer** and **approver** (NL-82; one person, two roles, labelled as such, PD-25). Claude gives Innocent the URL, both logins (from the seeded-account note, never in this issue), and the three lesson addresses: the WP-1 lesson (row 8, L4), the WP-2 lesson (row 9), the WP-3 lesson (row 25).
2. **Two stopwatch reviews (reviewer account).** For the WP-1 lesson and the WP-3 lesson: sign in as the reviewer, start a phone stopwatch the moment you open the lesson (that is `start_review`), review it as you would for real (read, fix what you would fix, resolve any confirmation), pause the stopwatch if you leave the screen, stop it at **Submit**. Keep one browser tab, in focus; do not leave the lesson open and idle. Tell Claude: lesson, stopwatch minutes, any pauses.
3. **Approve and rate three lessons (approver account).** Sign in as the approver (the account that did **not** last edit the lesson), open each of the three lessons from the review queue, read the evidence checklist, **Approve** on the pinned version and choose the rating: *usable as drafted* / *usable with edits* / *rewritten*. Rate honestly; a *rewritten* is a useful result (it becomes an error-analysis trace, PD-28). The WP-2 lesson needs a reviewer pass first (use the reviewer account for a normal short review, no stopwatch).
4. Say "sessions done" on the issue.

**What Claude records afterwards (never drives these sessions).** From stored rows only: per lesson, computed active minutes (`lesson_metrics.active_minutes`) beside the stopwatch minutes and the percentage difference; the three ratings; the board tile as shown (median, *n*, G-2, "approved k of N", caption). Claude does not edit a session, a heartbeat or a rating, and labels every number "one reviewer, n = k" (PD-25, GR-15). The synthetic NL-99 scripted-review rows stay in the database and stay excluded.
