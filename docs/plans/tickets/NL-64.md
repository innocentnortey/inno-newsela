### Story
US-16/US-18/US-20 — As a reviewer (P-1) I need to confirm or remove a tool-chosen standard (with an undo), start and submit review, withdraw, and ask for a regenerated candidate, with every disabled button saying why; my active time is recorded by heartbeat and shown only as unit totals (US-14 disclosure).

### What to build
- `src/features/workspace/actions/**` (replaces NL-24's `WorkspaceActionBar({ lesson, issues, clientState })`) and `use-review-heartbeat.ts` (`useReviewHeartbeat({ lessonId, versionId })`); the issue-rail **Confirm** / **Remove** buttons for `narrowed`/`added` standards and **Resolve…** / **Clear flag…** openers (the dialogs are NL-66).
- Footer bar: the disclosure "Active review time on this lesson is recorded and shown only as unit totals."; the blocking count; primary **Submit for approval** with its reasons beneath, each on its own line from CON-11/13 + `clientState`: "3 blocking issues" (`BLOCKED`), "1 standard to confirm" (`CONFIRMATION_OPEN`), "Accept or reject the AI suggestion first" (`SUGGESTION_PENDING`), "Saving" (`NOT_STORED`); `aria-disabled` with the reason, and pressing it moves focus to the first issue. A server refusal shows the `danger` banner "Submit refused: `RL.6.2` has no checking item on the stored copy." and nothing freezes. The **More** menu: Regenerate (optional note popover → `regenerateLesson`; "Drafting a candidate… The current lesson is unchanged." and a link to SCR-12 when ready), History (→ SCR-16), AI assist (only when cut #8 stands).
- Transitions via `transitionLesson` (CON-18): `start_review` on a reviewer's first open of a draft (UXP-1; quiet line "You're the reviewer for this lesson."; never for author/approver, UXQ-11), `submit` with `client_state_vector` from `clientState`, `withdraw` (no reason, UXP-10; toast "Lesson 4 withdrawn. It's back in review."). Success toast "Lesson 4 submitted for approval." and the stepper moves; frozen lessons offer only **Withdraw** and History (UXP-3).
- Standards: `decideStandard` (CON-14) acts at once with a 6 s Undo toast (UXQ-8); `UNDO_EXPIRED` closes the toast and re-reads the lesson; `COLLAB_UNAVAILABLE` says "Couldn't reach the editor server. Nothing changed. Try again."
- Heartbeat sender (CON-20, HP-8): `sendHeartbeat` every 15 s only while the lesson is open, the tab in focus and touched within 60 s; reviewers and approvers only (authors never send); a failed heartbeat is dropped silently; pauses on hidden tab; no timer text shown beyond the disclosure.
- Must not: call a transition the role cannot use, compute time client-side beyond the cadence, or decide eligibility (the server decides; this is presentation of its reasons).
