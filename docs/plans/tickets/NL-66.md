### Story
US-20/US-21 — As a reviewer (P-1), or an approver clearing a safety flag, I need to record a named resolution for a blocking issue fixed outside the tool, or clear a safety flag with a note, so the record says plainly what a person decided.

### What to build
- `src/features/resolve/resolve-dialog.tsx` (replaces NL-24's shell, same props `ResolveDialog({ lessonId, mode: "resolve" | "clear_flag", issueId?, flagId?, open, onClose })`): dialog `dialog-sm`. *Resolve*: title "Record a resolution", the issue sentence in a `well`, **Reason (required)** textarea with the helper example, "Counted as a resolution, not a fix.", Cancel · **Record resolution** → `recordResolution`. *Clear flag*: title "Clear safety flag", the flag and the linked teacher tip quoted, **Note (required)**, "Your name, note and the time are logged and shown on the approver's checklist.", Cancel · **Clear flag** → `clearSafetyFlag`.
- Errors: `REASON_REQUIRED` "A resolution needs a reason. Say what was done and where."; `NOTE_REQUIRED` "Clearing a safety flag needs a note. Say what you checked."; `NOT_RESOLVABLE` points to the action named in `details.use`; `LESSON_LOCKED`; network "Couldn't save. Your text is kept. Try again."; an author gets "Only reviewers and approvers can clear a flag." Success toasts "Issue resolved by J. Rivera." / "Safety flag cleared."; the issue moves to the Resolved group (NL-62's rail re-reads).
- A required field says "(required)" in its label and takes focus on open; an approver may clear a flag on a `submitted` lesson (CON-14); the flag in Lesson 31 whose warning sits in student-facing text shows no clear control (that case is "Move the warning out of the student text first", rendered by NL-62).
- Must not: send an empty reason or note (the server also refuses), mark a resolution as a fix, or write the note anywhere except the CON-14 call.
