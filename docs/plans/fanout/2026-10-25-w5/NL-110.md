## 1. Goal and sources

After a lesson is stored and its checks are done, a second claim loop (own lane, own poll) finds `(lesson_id, content_hash)` pairs that have no `judge_verdict` rows, runs NL-89's `JudgeJob.run` on each, and for each `no` writes one advisory `judge_no` issue (`level: advisory`, `blocks: []`, message labelled provisional). The judge never gates Submit or Approve. Stub judge only; no provider key is read.

2. **`PgJudgeWork`** (in `pg-pair-source.ts`):
   - `claim_next(): Promise<{lesson_id, content_hash} | null>`: in one `with_tx`: select the newest stored hash per lesson from `lesson_doc` (highest `seq`) whose `check_job` for that `(lesson_id, content_hash)` has `status='done'` and which has no `judge_verdict` row for `(lesson_id, content_hash)` and no live claim; take it with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` over `lesson_doc`; no new table. Claim marker without DDL: hold the row lock for the duration of the job inside the loop's own transaction is wrong (a judge call can take 30 s). Decision: lease via `pg_try_advisory_xact_lock`-free approach is also wrong across transactions. Use a session-level `pg_try_advisory_lock(hashtext(lesson_id || ':' || content_hash))` taken on a dedicated connection and released in `finally`; the SKIP-LOCKED `SELECT` picks candidates, the advisory lock decides ownership, and a second loop skips locked pairs. Idempotence still rests on the verdict-row existence check (re-running after a crash is a no-op once any verdict row exists; a half-judged hash gets `JudgeJob.run` again, and the unique index plus the existence check bounds it: see Open item 4).

Setting `created_at` to the done check's `finished_at` (not now) keeps `checked_hash_sql` stable. The message is fixed text: `"Provisional: the judge says this activity may not teach <code>. Advisory only; a person decides."` (no model output, no rationale; the rationale stays in `model_call.raw_output`).
