## Review fixes (commit after 0e573c1): boundary case for `no_silent_loss`, S5 label, notes

**Changed.**
- `no_silent_loss` now has a lock-boundary case in every trial. A's socket frames are held (as in `zero_edits_after_lock`), A (held) and B (live) both type for about 2 s, B takes the lock through the real transitions route 250 ms in (no page swaps to the read view), frames are released, A shows the refused notice. A token counts as typed when the editor was editable and focused when its first key went in (the old "ambiguous, not counted" rule is gone). Every typed token must be whole in the submit snapshot, whole in the `Removed text` notice, or split (head in the snapshot, tail opening the notice); anything else is `lost` and fails. Before, 181 counted and 0 were refused, so the refusal path was never exercised.
- `evals/src/suites/s5.ts`: case source now names the WP-3 flex-day compiled draft and why WP-1 Lesson 4 cannot be approved. `run.ts` cites S-125-1 (not S-23). `docs/plans/seam-followups.md` S-125-2: p95 verdict is on the net basis (O-1) with raw shown; NL-137 AC verify strings use `npx tsx e2e/integrity/run.ts --trials 20`.

**Checked.** `s5.test.ts` 7 pass; typecheck 0 errors; eslint on changed files clean. 1-trial smoke of all six assertions: five pass, `no_silent_loss` fails (below). `run.ts --trials 20 --grep no_silent_loss`: **6/20 pass, 14 fail**, report `evals/reports/integrity-0e573c14a27e.md`.

**Finding (not papered over).** In every failing trial exactly one token is lost, and it is always B's token that straddled the lock (`lost_at_boundary` 1, `lost` 1). B's editor turns read-only partway through the token: the keys already in go to the snapshot, one or two characters reach the server after the lock and show in B's notice (a "split" token, counted as accounted for), and the remaining keys land on an editor that is already read-only and are dropped with no trace in the notice (example: `n01` in the snapshot, `b` in the notice, `06` nowhere). Whether that is a product defect (read-only switch should surface or buffer late keys in the notice) or acceptable (the editor visibly refuses input) is a decision for the owner of the editor (NL-101) and Innocent; S5 `no_silent_loss` is floored at 1.00, so it stays red until then. Nothing in the assertion was loosened to hide it.
