## How to read it

- **Status** is copied from the source's own status column, never upgraded. `decided` = the record says decided
  (including "recommendation accepted" and "adopted under PD-32", Innocent's standing authorization of 2026-10-25).
  `proposed` = written, not accepted. `assumption` = the record calls it an assumption. Rows that are measured results
  or open findings, not decisions, say `n/a: measured ...` and are narrated as results, never as decisions. Where
  Innocent has not read a spike result, the row says so.
- **Type:** B business, T technical, R research finding or measured result that changed the plan or proves a claim.
- **Demo moment:** the step of the product demo the decision attaches to (working labels, not a script).
- **Rank:** 1 = a reviewer would probe it first (the brief's traps, the data boundary, the metric, the biggest cut).
- **Seconds:** speaking time for the decisions segment. The product demo comes first and stays under 5 minutes; the brief
  sets no video length, so the framing split is a decisions segment of **up to 300 s** unless Innocent sets another
  length (not yet set). `prep` = interview prep only, not in the video.
- Source paths are relative to the repo root. PD-N rows are in `docs/prd/newsela-prd.md`; T-N are traps in
  `docs/analysis/2026-10-24-brief-audit.md` section 5; HP-N are cards in `docs/design/hard-problems.md`.
