## How to read it

- **Status** is copied from the source's own status column, never upgraded. `decided` = the record says decided
  (including "recommendation accepted" and "adopted under PD-32", Innocent's standing authorization of 2026-10-25). `proposed` = written, not
  accepted. `assumption` = the record calls it an assumption. Where Innocent has not read a spike result, the row says so.
- **Type:** B business, T technical, R research finding that changed the plan.
- **Demo moment:** the step of the product demo the decision attaches to (names are working labels, not a script).
  The product demo comes first and is under 5 minutes; decisions are trimmed to fit, never the demo. The brief gives no
  video length, so the framing split is demo under 5 min, then a decisions segment of up to 5 min unless Innocent sets
  another length (not yet set).
- **Rank:** 1 = a reviewer would probe it first (the brief's traps, the data boundary, the metric, the biggest cut).
  Rows 1 to about 12 are the video candidates; the rest go to interview prep.
- Source paths are relative to the repo root. PD-N rows are in `docs/prd/newsela-prd.md`; T-N are traps in
  `docs/analysis/2026-10-24-brief-audit.md` §5; HP-N are cards in `docs/design/hard-problems.md`.
